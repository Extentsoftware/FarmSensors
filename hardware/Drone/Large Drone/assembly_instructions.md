# Assembly Instructions - Farm Surveillance Drone

## Overview
This document provides detailed step-by-step assembly instructions for the farm surveillance drone system. Follow these instructions carefully to ensure proper assembly and system integration.

## Safety Precautions

### General Safety
- **Work Area**: Clean, well-ventilated workspace
- **Personal Protective Equipment**: Safety glasses, gloves, dust masks
- **Fire Safety**: Fire extinguisher nearby, no open flames
- **Chemical Safety**: Proper handling of epoxy and solvents
- **Electrical Safety**: Disconnect power before working on electrical systems

### Tool Safety
- **Sharp Tools**: Handle with care, keep sharp
- **Power Tools**: Use appropriate safety guards
- **Hot Tools**: Allow to cool before handling
- **Chemical Tools**: Use in well-ventilated area

## Assembly Sequence

### Phase 1: Airframe Construction (Weeks 1-4)

#### Step 1: Wing Construction
**Materials Required:**
- Carbon fiber sheets (0.5mm)
- Carbon fiber tubes (20mm diameter)
- Balsa wood sheets (3mm)
- Epoxy resin system
- Vacuum bagging materials

**Tools Required:**
- Vacuum pump
- Heat gun
- Digital scale
- Calipers
- Sandpaper (various grits)

**Procedure:**

1. **Wing Mold Preparation**
   - Clean aluminum wing molds
   - Apply mold release wax
   - Install wing root and tip blocks
   - Verify mold alignment

2. **Spar Construction**
   - Cut carbon fiber tubes to length (3.6m)
   - Sand tube ends for bonding
   - Install spar caps (carbon fiber strips)
   - Apply epoxy and cure

3. **Rib Construction**
   - Cut balsa wood to rib templates
   - Sand ribs to final shape
   - Install lightening holes
   - Apply carbon fiber reinforcement

4. **Wing Layup**
   - Place carbon fiber skin in mold
   - Install spars and ribs
   - Apply epoxy resin
   - Install vacuum bagging system
   - Apply vacuum and cure at 120°C for 2 hours

5. **Wing Finishing**
   - Remove from mold
   - Trim excess material
   - Sand to final shape
   - Apply primer and paint

**Quality Check:**
- [ ] Wing weight: 3.5 kg ± 0.2 kg
- [ ] Wing span: 3.8m ± 0.01m
- [ ] Wing chord: 0.4m (root), 0.25m (tip)
- [ ] Surface finish: Smooth, no voids
- [ ] Structural integrity: No cracks or delamination
