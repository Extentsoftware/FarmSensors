# Regulatory Compliance Guide - Farm Surveillance Drone

## Overview
This guide provides comprehensive information on regulatory compliance requirements for operating a commercial surveillance drone system on a farm. Compliance is essential for legal operation and insurance coverage.

## FAA Part 107 Commercial Drone Operations

### Remote Pilot Certificate Requirements
- **Age**: Must be at least 16 years old
- **English Proficiency**: Must be able to read, write, and understand English
- **Physical/Mental Condition**: Must be in physical and mental condition to safely operate a drone
- **Knowledge Test**: Must pass the Part 107 knowledge test
- **Recurrent Training**: Must complete recurrent training every 24 months

### Part 107 Knowledge Test
- **Test Location**: FAA-approved testing centers
- **Test Cost**: $175 (as of 2024)
- **Test Duration**: 2 hours
- **Passing Score**: 70% or higher
- **Test Content**: 
  - Airspace regulations
  - Weather and meteorology
  - Aircraft performance
  - Emergency procedures
  - Crew resource management
  - Radio communications
  - Airport operations
  - Maintenance and inspection

### Study Resources
- **FAA Study Guide**: Available on FAA website
- **Online Courses**: Commercial drone training courses
- **Practice Tests**: Online practice tests available
- **Study Time**: 20-40 hours recommended

## Drone Registration

### Registration Requirements
- **Weight**: Drones over 0.55 lbs (250g) must be registered
- **Registration Type**: Commercial operations require Part 107 registration
- **Registration Cost**: $5 per drone (3-year registration)
- **Registration Process**: Online through FAA DroneZone

### Registration Information Required
- **Owner Information**: Name, address, phone number
- **Drone Information**: Make, model, serial number
- **Registration Number**: Must be displayed on drone
- **Renewal**: Every 3 years

## Insurance Requirements

### Commercial Liability Insurance
- **Minimum Coverage**: $1 million per occurrence
- **Aggregate Coverage**: $2 million total
- **Coverage Types**:
  - Bodily injury
  - Property damage
  - Personal injury
  - Advertising injury
- **Cost**: $2,000-8,000 per year

### Hull Insurance (Optional)
- **Coverage**: Physical damage to drone
- **Coverage Amount**: Full replacement value
- **Deductible**: $1,000-5,000
- **Cost**: $1,000-3,000 per year

### Insurance Providers
- **Aviation Insurance**: Specialized aviation insurance companies
- **General Liability**: General liability insurance with drone coverage
- **Farm Insurance**: Farm insurance with drone coverage
- **Comparison**: Get quotes from multiple providers

## Airspace Authorization

### Controlled Airspace
- **Class B, C, D Airspace**: Requires airspace authorization
- **LAANC**: Low Altitude Authorization and Notification Capability
- **Authorization Process**: Apply through FAA DroneZone
- **Response Time**: Usually within minutes for LAANC
- **Altitude Limits**: Usually 400 feet AGL maximum

### Uncontrolled Airspace
- **Class G Airspace**: Generally no authorization required
- **Altitude Limits**: 400 feet AGL maximum
- **Weather Requirements**: Visual flight rules (VFR)
- **Visibility Requirements**: 3 statute miles minimum

### Special Use Airspace
- **Restricted Areas**: May require special authorization
- **Prohibited Areas**: Generally off-limits
- **Military Operations Areas**: Check for military activity
- **Temporary Flight Restrictions**: Check for TFRs
