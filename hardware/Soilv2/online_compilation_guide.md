# Online Asymptote Compilation Guide

## Step 1: Go to Online Compiler
Visit: https://asymptote.ualberta.ca/

## Step 2: Compile Circuit Diagram
1. Open `circuit_diagram.asy` in your text editor
2. Copy all the content
3. Paste into the online Asymptote editor
4. Click "Compile"
5. Download the generated PDF

## Step 3: Compile Electrode Design Diagram
1. Open `electrode_design_diagram.asy` in your text editor
2. Copy all the content
3. Paste into the online Asymptote editor
4. Click "Compile"
5. Download the generated PDF

## Alternative Online Compilers
- https://asymptote.sourceforge.io/asyweb/
- https://www.overleaf.com/ (supports Asymptote)

## What You'll Get
- **circuit_diagram.pdf**: Complete circuit schematic
- **electrode_design_diagram.pdf**: Detailed electrode layout and cross-sections
